### Areas inspected and left alone

- **Bundling the SDK into `dist/` with esbuild** (npm would never see the SDK's `package.json`: no postinstall, no qmd, no auto-installed peers, smaller install). Not tested in this run; it changes the build shape (Ink/React/yoga-wasm inlined or external, `sharp` external, the `dist/src/index.*` re-export the patch relies on becomes a build-time concern) and interacts with every `spike/` suite that imports `@strands-agents/sdk` directly. Recorded as gated-out below with the untested-evidence rating, not dropped.
- **Publishing a patched SDK fork under a scoped name.** Doubles the maintenance of every SDK bump (the patch is already regenerated per version via `pnpm patch-commit`) and hides the patch from `pnpm` users who read `pnpm-workspace.yaml`. Not proposed.
- **Asking upstream to move `@tobilu/qmd` from `optionalDependencies` to an optional peer.** Worth an issue, but it is not a darwin iteration and cannot be accepted by this repository's checks; noted in the direction's Notes only.
- **`pnpm publish` vs `npm publish`.** Either works: the patch lives in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, not `package.json`, so the published manifest is the same; `pnpm publish` also honours `files`. Left to the implementer.
