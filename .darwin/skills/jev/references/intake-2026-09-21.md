## Corrections and limits before reuse

- OpenRouter uses `/api/alpha/decisions` and `typesafe/jev-1.13`, not a chat
  completion achieved just by changing a model name. Direct TypeSafe uses
  `/v1/systemone`, `jev-1.13.0`, and its own key. See [API](api.md).
- Choice has at most 255 options per question in the inspected contract. A page
  with thousands of links needs host filtering or staged decisions, not an
  invented unlimited-Choice API. See [official contract](https://docs.typesafe.ai/api).
- “Always typed” is not “always correct”; Noul has no separate confidence field.
  Latency, pricing and benchmark wins are workload-dependent, not universal.
- The [PrimeLine original](https://primeline.cc/blog/typesafe-jev-pre-registered-test)
  discusses discarded/rebuilt experiments, different rankings on its two jobs,
  and author/model-influenced labels. Its selective-prediction observations do
  not establish general calibration or an independent double-blind victory.
  We do not repeat the roundup's stronger characterization.
- `pg-jev` is a PostgreSQL extension; the previously listed `jevql` evaluates
  semantic expressions in a CLI before ordinary Postgres queries. Do not merge
  these implementations or their setup requirements.
- Demo footage and reported benchmark numbers are not production-readiness
  evidence. AI-text classifications must not decide authorship or misconduct;
  résumé handling here organizes explicit evidence, not autonomous rejection.
